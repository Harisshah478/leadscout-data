About LeadScout Data

I run LeadScout Data, a one-person data extraction service. I turn scattered public listings into clean, structured prospect lists that are actually ready to use.

Most "lead lists" people buy are outdated, incomplete, or full of duplicates. I do this differently: every dataset I hand over is pulled fresh, field by field, from public sources and sales intelligence platforms like Google Maps, LinkedIn, Apollo.io, ZoomInfo and Clay — then checked and deduplicated by hand before it reaches you.

Whether you need 50 highly targeted contacts in one city or a broad list across an entire industry, I scope the extraction to exactly what you're targeting: industry, location, company size, or a specific keyword/niche.
