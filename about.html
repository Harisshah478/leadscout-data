About

Data built around your target audience.

LeadScout Data helps you find and reach the right prospects — through targeted data, LinkedIn outreach and email marketing.

Finding the right prospects is often more difficult than reaching them. Business information can be spread across directories, company websites, social platforms and sales intelligence databases.

LeadScout Data brings relevant information together, structures it into a consistent format and removes duplicate records so you can spend less time cleaning spreadsheets and more time working your pipeline.

Each project is scoped around the audience you want to reach. You can define the industry, location, company size, niche, keywords and the fields required for your workflow.

Whether you need a focused list for one city or a larger dataset covering an entire industry, the goal is the same: deliver useful, organized data that matches your targeting requirements.
